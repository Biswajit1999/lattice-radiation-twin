## Search log

Queries included every requested identifier/title; HST + Gaia + Euclid + radiation model; cross-mission radiation CTI; hierarchical radiation HST detector; space weather CTI Gaia proton GCR; astronomical detector digital twin radiation forecasting; latent radiation detector models; trap pumping; ArCTIc; Pyxel CCD273; SRH. GitHub searches `HST Gaia Euclid radiation` and `CTI radiation state space` returned no repositories on the access date. Narrow negative results are not proof of absence. Acronym collisions were excluded.

Environment sources: [NASA OMNIWeb](https://omniweb.gsfc.nasa.gov/ow.html), [NOAA legacy GOES](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/goes-1-15/space-weather-instruments), [GOES-R SEISS](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/goes-r-space-environment-in-situ). OMNI supplies near-Earth proton fluxes and geomagnetic/solar indices, not detector dose. GCR and SEP damage mechanisms are discussed by the mission papers; independent GCR measurements and shielding transport remain unimplemented.

## Candidate integration gap

A targeted literature and repository search did not identify an open framework combining all of the following: reproducible HST longitudinal extraction; distinct LEO/L2 exposure kernels; mission-specific hierarchical observations; locked forward-chaining baselines; published-data access gates for Gaia/Euclid; and posterior propagation to pixel-level science bias with versioned falsification. This is a hypothesis, not a priority claim. The recent HST paper already joins mission comparison, dosimetry and the implausible-fit warning. Another hot-pixel demo would not close the gap.

## Access limitations

A&A DOI resolver failures were worked around using author manuscripts, arXiv and indexed primary publisher text. The Hall pumping paper was identified but not fully read. A live 2026-09-12 query of all 248 official Gaia TAP tables found no exact CTI, charge-injection, calibration or engineering table token; the 26-epoch series is therefore represented only by explicitly published constraints. Euclid trap-pumping machine-readable series was not located; absence is not proven. [ESA Q1 contents](https://www.cosmos.esa.int/web/euclid/q1-contents) lists calibrated VIS frames; actual retrieval is a separate gate. No figures digitised.
